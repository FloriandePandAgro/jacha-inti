PHASE 2Throughout the round

Not at the end: as you go, cage by cage.

  • Wash each bowl — on the spot, as soon as you pick it up.
  • Take them to the frutero — that is where they get filled with food afterwards.
  • Check everyone's water — at every cage you pass. This is the one people forget.
  • Remove old food — every time you see it.
  • In every cage, no exceptions : clean, rake, pick up leftover food and hose down. And take out anything that is not pleasant for the animals.
PHASE 3End of the morning

The last two stops of the round, after the bowls.

  • Tapir area — 3 bowls: 2 for the geese and 1 for a rodent, plus the tapir's own. Four in all.4
  • Llamas — fill a wheelbarrow with whatever is given that day (leaves, vegetables or whatever they hand you), take it to the llamas and leave the food there.1

That is the end of the main morning round.
